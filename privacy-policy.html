Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Your information stays private. Phone numbers, email addresses, and any other details you give us are kept confidential. Only Rylo Platforms uses them. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Rylo Platforms ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, use our chat widget, fill out a form, or book a call, and how we use and protect that information.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Business details and any messages you send us through our forms, chat, or booking pages

We may also collect basic, non-personal technical data (such as browser type and pages visited) to help our website run properly.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your questions and messages
  • Schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments
  • Send you information about our services that you asked for
  • Provide customer support

We Do Not Share Your Information

Your phone number, email address, and other personal information are never sold, rented, traded, or shared with outside parties. Access is limited to Rylo Platforms.

The only exceptions are trusted service providers that help us run our business, such as our scheduling, messaging, and website hosting tools. They process your information only on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for anything else. We may also share information if required by law.

SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from Rylo Platforms about your inquiry and appointments. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Email Communications

If you give us your email address, we may email you about your inquiry or appointment. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email or by contacting us.

How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to keep your information secure and to prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Your Choices

You can ask us at any time to view, update, or delete the personal information we have about you. Just contact us using the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

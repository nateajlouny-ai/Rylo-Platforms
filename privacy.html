SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in (for example, through our website chat widget or booking calendar), you agree to receive text messages from Rylo Platforms about our contractor growth services, responses to your inquiries, and appointment scheduling and reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the Text Message services.

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving text messages. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. For more details, see the SMS Terms in our Terms of Service.
